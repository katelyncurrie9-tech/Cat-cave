## Comparable Companies

### 1. Syrup Tech — Closest Comparable
**Use Case:** AI-powered demand forecasting and inventory optimization for fashion/apparel retailers
**Headquarters:** New York, USA
**Founded:** 2020

| Metric | Detail |
|---|---|
| Seed Round | $6.3M — led by Google Gradient Ventures |
| Series A | $17.5M — led by Accel Partners (Dec 2023) |
| Total Raised | ~$25M |
| Implied Valuation | ~$100M at Series A |
| Key Investors | Accel, Google Gradient Ventures, 1984 Ventures |
| Notable Customers | Desigual, Reformation, PKZ |

**Why it's relevant to Thri5:**
- Directly comparable product: AI-driven inventory forecasting for mid-market retailers
- Same structural problem: stockouts + excess inventory = $250B economic drag in apparel/retail
- Raised Series A with a handful of live customers — similar stage to where Thri5 is heading post-pilot
- Google's AI-focused fund (Gradient Ventures) as lead signals domain credibility

**Key takeaway:** Syrup got to $100M valuation on ~$25M raised with a focused mid-market retail vertical. If Thri5 can show comparable pilot metrics and a pipeline of 3–5 named prospects, a similar trajectory is supportable.

## Summary for Lending Conversation

**Bull case:** Syrup Tech validated a nearly identical product in the same market and got to $100M valuation on $25M raised. Thri5, with strong backers and a sticky live pilot, is on the same trajectory 2–3 years behind Syrup. The market is growing at 30% CAGR and $428M+ flowed into AI retail in a six-month window alone.

**Bear case / key diligence:** Thri5 is one pilot with no revenue history. The Syrup comp only holds if (a) the pilot converts to a paid contract, (b) the pipeline has 3–5 credible follow-on opportunities, and (c) the investor syndicate includes at least one retail-domain specialist (analogous to Accel's role in Syrup).

**Recommendation:** Request pipeline breakdown by stage + pilot KPI scorecard before sizing any facility. Use Syrup's Series A metrics as the benchmark for what "ready to underwrite" looks like.
