## Responses to Your Additional Questions

**1. Can Claude / OpenClaw or other general orchestration tools do what Thri5 does?**

General-purpose AI orchestration tools (LangChain, OpenAI Assistants, Claude, etc.) *can* be configured to handle retail data tasks, but they require significant customization, retail-domain expertise, and ongoing model maintenance that most mid-market retailers cannot do in-house. Thri5's value proposition is the pre-built, retail-specific workflow + proprietary training data accumulated across clients — that is genuinely difficult for a generic tool to replicate out of the box. The risk is that larger retailers with strong tech teams build internally, but Thri5's target market (mid-market, lean IT) structurally cannot. Short answer: general tools are a theoretical substitute, not a practical one for their target customer.

Mid-market grocery, fashion, and specialty retail is a defensible wedge.
