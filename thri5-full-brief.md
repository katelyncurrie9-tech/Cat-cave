## 1. The Market — Why Now

The global AI in retail market is valued at **$12–31B today** and is projected to reach **$40–165B by 2030**, growing at **23–32% CAGR** across all major research sources. This is not speculative — **~90% of retailers** are actively using or formally evaluating AI, **92% of U.S. retailers** increased AI investment in 2025, and **94%** report it has already reduced operational costs.

The ROI case is concrete and measurable:

| Use Case | Impact |
|---|---|
| Demand forecasting / inventory | Addresses a $250B overstock/stockout problem in retail |
| AI-powered pricing | Margin improvement without raising prices on consumers |
| Customer service automation | Handles 60–70% of requests automatically |
| Labor / checkout optimization | 9+ staff hours saved per store per week |
| Personalization | Up to 15% conversion rate lift |

Bain reports AI adoption drives a **9% bottom-line improvement** on average and cuts cost of goods sold by **1–2 percentage points** — material for any thin-margin retailer.

**Recession resilience:** This category behaves defensively in a downturn. Retailers facing margin pressure have two levers — raise prices (increasingly impossible) or cut costs (AI is now the most efficient tool available). During COVID, retailers that digitized fastest came out with structural cost advantages. A mild recession strengthens the ROI case for Thri5; it doesn't weaken it. The real macro risk is that sales cycles lengthen — not that the product becomes irrelevant. Tariff and supply chain disruption similarly *increases* the value of demand forecasting tools, not decreases it.

## 2. What Thri5 Does — And Why It's Sticky

Thri5 is an AI-powered retail optimization platform that helps retailers reduce margin leakage through demand forecasting, inventory management, and personalized pricing recommendations.

**Why customers don't leave:**

- **Deep system integration:** Thri5 embeds directly into a retailer's POS, ERP, and e-commerce infrastructure. Replacing it requires significant operational disruption and data migration — high switching cost by design.
- **Data flywheel:** The platform improves continuously as it ingests more of each retailer's transaction data. The longer a retailer uses Thri5, the more accurate and valuable the models become — a compounding advantage that widens over time.
- **Workflow dependency:** Merchandising and planning teams rely on Thri5's daily recommendations as part of their core decision-making. It becomes mission-critical, not optional.

**Why generic AI can't replace it:**
A common challenge to any AI retail company is: *"Can't retailers just use ChatGPT or build this internally?"* The answer is no, for three reasons:

1. General-purpose AI models are not trained on retail transaction data, seasonal inventory patterns, or SKU-level demand signals. Thri5's are. That specialization takes years of proprietary data to build.
2. Mid-market retailers ($50M–$500M revenue) do not have data science teams capable of building and maintaining custom AI models. Thri5 is the outsourced capability they could never build themselves.
3. Thri5's value is not just the model — it is the pre-built connectors to retail tech stacks, the merchandising workflow layer, and the ongoing model retraining. That represents 3–5 years of engineering that no general tool provides out of the box.
